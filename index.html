I'm Shahin Khalil, an AI video director based in Baku. I build whole films with generative tools — writing the prompts, generating the shots, cutting the edit and grading the final frame.

What started as an experiment with text-to-video turned into a full pipeline. Today I make music videos, brand spots and short films for clients who want something that doesn't look like everything else on the feed. Every project starts with a script and a look book, and ends with a file that's ready to publish.
